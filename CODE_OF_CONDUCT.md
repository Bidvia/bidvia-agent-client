# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to keep this project respectful, constructive, and inclusive.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- being considerate in feedback and review
- focusing on the technical issue instead of the person
- assuming good intent while still holding a high bar for evidence and correctness

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- bad-faith derailment of technical discussions
- publishing private or sensitive information without permission

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate these expectations.
